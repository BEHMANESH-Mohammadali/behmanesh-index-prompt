# Glossary

## Behmanesh Structural Index (BSI)

A framework-oriented engineering architecture designed to improve structural fidelity during the execution of formal analytical methodologies by Large Language Models.

---

## Interpretation Drift

Structural deviation occurring when execution no longer faithfully implements the original framework specification.

---

## Framework Fidelity

The degree to which execution preserves the procedural structure, constraints, and intended methodology defined by a formal framework.

---

## Epistemic Integrity

The preservation of analytical meaning, structural consistency, and methodological correctness throughout execution.

---

## Execution Engine

The architectural component responsible for implementing formal specifications while preserving framework fidelity.

---

## Structural Validation

Independent evaluation of execution structure without relying solely on semantic correctness.

---

## Specification

The formal analytical methodology that defines objectives, procedures, constraints, and evaluation criteria.

---

## Execution

The process by which an LLM applies a specification to produce analytical reasoning.

---

## Validation

Independent assessment of whether execution remained faithful to the original specification.
