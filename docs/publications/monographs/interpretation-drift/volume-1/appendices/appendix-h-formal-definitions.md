# Appendix H — Formal Definitions

## Purpose

This appendix establishes concise normative definitions for the principal
architectural concepts introduced throughout Volume I.

---

## Execution Governance

Execution Governance is the architectural discipline responsible for preserving
faithful execution of formally specified analytical procedures.

---

## Framework Fidelity

Framework Fidelity is the degree to which execution conforms to an explicitly
defined analytical specification.

---

## Interpretation Drift

Interpretation Drift denotes measurable deviation between the intended
analytical specification and the execution actually produced.

---

## Specification Preservation

Specification Preservation denotes maintenance of architectural intent across
all execution stages without semantic substitution.

---

## Execution Traceability

Execution Traceability denotes the ability to reconstruct every architectural
decision throughout execution.

---

## Structural Consistency

Structural Consistency denotes preservation of architectural relationships
between all specification components.
