# Appendix I — Architectural Axioms

## Axiom 1

Specification precedes execution.

## Axiom 2

Execution shall not redefine specification.

## Axiom 3

Architectural evidence shall not become architectural objective.

## Axiom 4

Governance operates upon execution rather than internal model mechanisms.

## Axiom 5

Framework Fidelity is evaluated relative to explicit specifications only.

## Axiom 6

Architectural evolution shall preserve backward consistency unless formally
revised.
