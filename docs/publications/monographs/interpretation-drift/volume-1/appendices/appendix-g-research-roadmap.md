# Appendix G — Research Roadmap

## Purpose

This appendix documents the planned continuation of the Behmanesh Structural
Index research program.

The items listed below are intentionally excluded from Volume I and therefore
shall not be interpreted as completed contributions of this volume.

Volume I establishes only the conceptual architecture of Execution Governance.

---

# Research Program

## Phase 1 — Formal Operationalization

Future work will develop formal operational definitions for:

- Framework Fidelity
- Interpretation Drift
- Execution Traceability
- Specification Preservation
- Structural Consistency

---

## Phase 2 — Mathematical Formalization

Future work will introduce mathematical formulations for:

- Framework Fidelity
- Interpretation Drift
- Epistemic Integrity Gap (EIG)
- Execution Consistency Check (ECC)
- Governance Stability

---

## Phase 3 — Controlled Experimental Evaluation

Controlled experimental studies are planned using:

- identical analytical tasks
- BSI-enabled execution
- baseline execution
- blind evaluators
- statistical analysis

---

## Phase 4 — Cross-LLM Validation

The architecture will be evaluated across multiple language models,
including future generations, to assess architectural generality rather
than model-specific behavior.

---

## Phase 5 — Cross-Domain Validation

Future evaluations will include:

- scientific reasoning
- software engineering
- legal analysis
- medicine
- policy analysis
- strategic planning

---

## Phase 6 — Quantitative EIG

Future research will transform EIG into a measurable engineering metric
capable of evaluating execution integrity quantitatively.

---

## Phase 7 — Automated Execution Governance

Future work includes implementation of:

- SOP Pipeline
- Guardrail Pipeline
- ECC Engine
- EIG Engine
- Specification Verification Engine

---

## Phase 8 — Specification Compiler

Future research will investigate automatic compilation of formal analytical
frameworks into executable governance specifications.

---

## Phase 9 — Pattern Library

Development of reusable repositories for:

- successful execution patterns
- execution failures
- Interpretation Drift patterns
- recovery mechanisms

---

## Phase 10 — Formal Verification

Future work will investigate formal verification methods for execution
governance architectures.

---

## Phase 11 — Reproducibility Benchmark

Future benchmark datasets will evaluate:

- Framework Fidelity
- Specification Preservation
- Reproducibility
- Traceability
- Execution Consistency

---

## Phase 12 — Reference Implementation

A complete open-source implementation of the Behmanesh Structural Index
execution-governance architecture is planned for future volumes.

---

## Architectural Note

None of the research directions documented in this appendix constitute
claims, results, validations, or demonstrated capabilities of Volume I.

They are recorded solely to document the intended continuation of the
research program while preserving the conceptual scope of this volume.

---

## Architectural Dependency Graph

The research program follows cumulative architectural dependencies.

Conceptual architecture precedes formal specification.

Formal specification precedes measurement.

Measurement precedes experimentation.

Experimentation precedes implementation.

Implementation precedes operational deployment.

Skipping dependency levels is discouraged because it weakens architectural
traceability.

## Long-Term Publication Vision

The complete publication program is expected to evolve through successive
volumes that progressively introduce:

- formal mathematical models;
- controlled empirical validation;
- reference software implementations;
- benchmark datasets;
- reproducibility studies;
- governance tooling;
- industrial deployment guidance.

Each future volume is intended to preserve the architectural specification
established in Volume I while expanding technical depth.

## Publication Policy

Each future volume should satisfy three publication requirements before
release:

- architectural consistency with previous volumes;
- explicit documentation of newly introduced concepts;
- preservation of terminology defined by the architectural baseline.

This policy aims to ensure cumulative rather than fragmented development of
the Behmanesh Structural Index.
