# Citation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how future volumes should reference architectural
components introduced in Volume I.

---

## Citation Principles

Architectural concepts should be cited consistently throughout all future
publications.

The first appearance of every core concept should reference its defining
chapter whenever applicable.

---

## Stable References

The following concepts constitute stable architectural references:

- Execution Governance
- Framework Fidelity
- Interpretation Drift
- Specification Preservation
- Structural Consistency
- Epistemic Integrity Gap

---

## Evolution Policy

Future terminology extensions shall preserve backward conceptual
compatibility.

Historical citations should remain valid across future editions whenever
possible.
