# Formal Definitions

## Purpose

This document records the canonical definitions adopted throughout Volume I.

---

## Execution Governance

The architectural process responsible for preserving specification-consistent
execution.

## Framework Fidelity

The degree to which execution remains consistent with an explicitly declared
analytical specification.

## Interpretation Drift

Observable deviation between declared analytical specification and produced
execution.

## Specification Preservation

Maintenance of architectural intent throughout execution.

## Structural Consistency

Consistency among architectural components across the complete specification.

Future volumes may refine these definitions without altering their conceptual
intent.
