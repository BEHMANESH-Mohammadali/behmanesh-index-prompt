## Governance Lifecycle

Execution governance should be interpreted as a continuous engineering
activity rather than a single verification event.

Specification review, terminology review, consistency auditing, and execution
evaluation should therefore be repeated throughout the lifecycle of the
architecture.

Continuous governance increases long-term specification reliability.
